# A considered path from arrival to inquiry

The refined experience keeps the ALV design familiar while giving each stage a clear role. The architectural threshold introduces the brand; the calm founder statement explains its approach. The collection keeps photographs, prices and specifications together while its progress and controls show where visitors are in the selection.

Category previews and distinct market entrances help visitors choose a direction. The services accordion makes the buying process easier to explore one step at a time. Short page transitions maintain continuity as visitors move from discovery to contact. Mobile and reduced-motion visitors receive the same content and actions without a pinned journey.

The inquiry form gives accurate feedback: it prepares an email draft and tells the visitor what remains to be sent. It does not imply ALV has received anything before delivery.

These choices are intended to make discovery, understanding and inquiry more straightforward. No conversion increase is claimed without measurement. The supplied logo is now used in the header and footer.

The owner video and uncut scroll show real browser behavior. This environment disables WebGL, so they show the graceful fallback; the implemented 3D arrival still needs a visual check on a GPU-enabled device. The source, private preview, motion map, comparisons, asset ledger and measured QA results are included for review.
